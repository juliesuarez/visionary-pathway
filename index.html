Who we are

Our Journey & Vision

From Bukasa Girl Care Group to a youth-empowerment organisation.

Our History

Visionary PathWays, formerly Bukasa Girl Care Group (BGCG), was initiated in October 2019 by a group of teenage girls and community elders concerned about the challenges facing disadvantaged urban girls in Bukasa, Kijwa zone and Namuwongo. We were officially formed on 7 August 2019 and registered on 8 September 2021.

On our 5th anniversary in August 2024, recognising that both young women and young men face critical economic and health challenges, we rebranded to Visionary PathWays to serve all young people aged 15 to 30.

Our Goal

To support young people through capacity-building activities, knowledge, skills, networks and motivation that improve living standards, dignity and self-esteem.

Who We Serve

Youth, people with disabilities, teenage mothers, school dropouts, survivors of gender-based violence, and vulnerable girls in Kanyogoga, Soweto and surrounding communities.

Our Vision

A generation of empowered, healthy, and self-reliant young people driving positive change in their communities.

Our Mission

Empowering young people with knowledge, skills, and opportunities in sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR) and entrepreneurship, enabling them to make informed decisions and build sustainable livelihoods.

Our Core Values

Integrity Perseverance Accountability Authenticity Community

Our Objectives

  • Promote inclusive SRHR education through age-appropriate, gender-sensitive information.
  • Improve access to youth-friendly SRHR services that are safe and confidential.
  • Empower youth through entrepreneurship for self-reliance and economic growth.
  • Promote gender equality by advocating for equal opportunities and inclusion.
  • Strengthen leadership and life skills in confident, responsible youth leaders.
What we do

Our Core Programs

Targeted initiatives designed to uplift, educate and empower young people.

🤱

Rise Her Initiative

Supports teenage mothers through psychosocial support, SRHR education, entrepreneurship and vocational skills training, start-up capital, mentorship, and linkages to supportive agencies, restoring dignity and promoting economic independence.

Read Nansikombi's story →
🚀

Rise Me Program

Empowers youth aged 15–30 through leadership development, SRHR education, entrepreneurship training and digital engagement, fostering the next generation of community leaders.

🏫

Pathfinders School

A school-based program in primary and secondary schools covering age-appropriate SRHR, life skills, leadership, mental health awareness, career guidance, and the formation of Visionary Clubs.

🎙️

Visionary Monthly Talks

Monthly interactive X Spaces where leaders and experts speak, inspire and engage directly with young people on youth-centred topics and advocacy.

Our impact

Impact & Achievements

Real, measurable change in Bukasa, Kanyogoga and Soweto.

Livelihoods

Vocational Skills Training

Trained over 30 girls in reusable sanitary pad making, and 20 girls in paper bag and briquette production: eco-friendly skills for alternative livelihoods.

Maternal Health

Maternal Health & Support

Raised UGX 370,000 in our 'Wash for Her, Raise Her' campaign and distributed 50 Mama Kits to teenage mothers for safe deliveries. Provided start-up capital and school fees for teenage mothers.

Community

Community Outreaches

12 community outreaches on SRHR and entrepreneurship. Official partners in the Annual Cloth Namuwongo Project (cleaning, tree planting, donation drives). Provided 20 bicycles to Community Health Workers.
